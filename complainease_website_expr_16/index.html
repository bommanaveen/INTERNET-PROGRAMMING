Common issues & solutions

How do I track my complaint?

Use the complaint ID shown after submission, or search by title in the status board.

Can I add more evidence later?

Open a complaint card and use “Add information” to attach a note or additional details.

What do the status labels mean?

Submitted means received; In Review means assigned; In Progress means work is underway; Resolved means the officer has marked it complete.

How do I contact the administrator?

Use the contact details in the Admin contact panel. Contact information shown here is sample data.
