Frequently asked questions

How do I book a ticket?

Search your route and date, choose a bus, select seats, enter passenger details, and confirm the demo payment.

Can I cancel my booking?

Open your dashboard and cancel any confirmed booking before the journey starts. This is a demonstration policy.

Is payment really processed?

No. Payment is simulated. A live system needs a secure payment gateway and server-side verification.

Can I leave a comment?

Yes. After booking, use your dashboard to leave a comment for that bus.
